Amalfi Coast boat tours with kids

Children usually love the boat and hate the waiting. Pick a format with short legs, shade and somewhere to swim.

Updated November 2026 · By the Amalfi Coast Boat Guide team

With children under about eight, choose a private boat for three to four hours along the coast (about €450–900 for the whole boat) or a two hour sunset cruise (€50–95 per person). From about nine, most children enjoy a full Capri day, ideally on a small-group or private boat with shade.

Best options by age

AgeBest choice
Babies and toddlersShort private coast cruise or sunset cruise
4–8 yearsPrivate half day, or Capri island tour if already on Capri
9+ yearsFull-day Capri tour, private if budget allows

How long is too long?

A full Capri day means seven to eight hours, with close to two hours of sailing in total. That works for most children from about nine who like the water. Younger ones usually do best with two to four hours and a swim in the middle. The giveaway is waiting time: queues at the Blue Grotto and crowded Marina Grande wear children out faster than the sea does.

Kid-friendly boat trips by where you stay

  • Positano: the sunset cruise to Li Galli is short and calm, a good first boat trip. For a longer day, a private coast cruise lets you stop at a quiet cove whenever someone needs a swim or a snack.
  • Amalfi: a private half day to the Emerald Grotto and the Furore fjord stays in sheltered water close to the shore. Route and prices.
  • Sorrento: Capri is under an hour away, the shortest crossing on the coast, so a Capri tour from Sorrento is the easiest full day with older kids.
  • Already on Capri: the one to two hour island tour from Marina Grande is a gentle option for any age.

What to ask the operator

  • Are there life jackets in children's sizes?
  • Does the boat have a shade canopy?
  • Is there a toilet on board? (Small gozzo boats usually do not.)
  • Can the plan be shortened if a child is unwell?

Seasickness

Mornings are generally calmer. Sit at the back, look at the horizon, avoid heavy breakfasts. Ask a pharmacist about remedies suitable for your child's age before the trip.

What to pack

Hats, swim shirts, reef-safe sunscreen, water, snacks, a change of clothes, a towel and swim shoes for pebble beaches.

Why private boats often make sense

For a family of four or five the price gap narrows, and the freedom to stop, swim and turn back is what makes the day work with kids.

Frequently asked questions

Are boat tours on the Amalfi Coast safe for babies?
Short trips in calm water are generally fine. Bring an infant life jacket if the operator does not have one, and keep the trip short.
Do children pay full price?
Policies vary. Shared tours often discount children; private boats charge per boat, not per person.
What is the best family boat tour from Positano?
A private three to four hour cruise along the coast, or the sunset cruise for younger children.
What age is good for a Capri boat tour?
Most children from about nine cope well with a full Capri day. Younger children can do it on a private boat that can shorten the plan, but a half day on the coast is usually happier for everyone.
Is there a toilet on Amalfi Coast tour boats?
Larger tour boats and yachts usually have one, small wooden gozzo boats usually do not. Ask before booking if you travel with young children.
