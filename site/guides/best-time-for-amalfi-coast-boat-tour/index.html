Best time for an Amalfi Coast boat tour

Boats run from about April to October, but May and September are a different experience from August.

Updated November 2026 · By the Amalfi Coast Boat Guide team

Late May, June and September are the sweet spot: the sea is around 21–24 °C, days are long and Capri is less crowded than in July and August. The season runs from about April to October. Book a morning departure for calmer sea and shorter Blue Grotto queues.

Month by month

MonthWhat to expect
AprilSeason starts, cool water, fewer departures, greener cliffs.
MayPleasant days, water still fresh for swimming, good prices.
JuneLong days, warm water, busy but manageable. Our favourite.
July–AugustHottest, warmest sea, highest prices, crowded Capri and Blue Grotto queues.
SeptemberWarm sea, softer light, crowds easing. Excellent.
OctoberStill possible, shorter days, more weather cancellations.
November–MarchVery few tours; private boats on request in good weather.

Time of day

Mornings are usually calmer. Afternoon breezes can make the return choppy. For photos, the late afternoon light on Positano is the best of the day.

Weather and cancellations

Skippers cancel when the sea is unsafe; reputable operators rebook or refund. Book your boat day early in your stay and choose listings with free cancellation so a rough day costs you nothing.

Best time for Capri and the Blue Grotto

The Blue Grotto needs a flat sea, so June and September give the best odds: calm days are common and the queue of rowing boats is shorter than in August. Aim to be there in the morning. A private boat can leave early and arrive before the shared tours; on a shared Capri tour, ask what time the boat usually reaches the grotto. More in our Blue Grotto guide.

Best time to charter a yacht or private boat

For a private gozzo or a yacht, June and September are the best value: warm water, steady weather and more boats free than in high summer. In July and August the best boats are booked weeks ahead and prices are at their peak. May and early October work too if you can accept cooler water and a higher chance of losing a day to wind.

Booking ahead

For June to September, book two to six weeks ahead for the best small-group and private boats. For May 2027 departures, prices and availability are already live on the partner sites.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sea temperature on the Amalfi Coast?
Roughly 18 °C in May, 23 °C in June, 25–26 °C in August and 23–24 °C in September.
What happens if my boat tour is cancelled for weather?
You are normally offered a new date or a refund. Check the cancellation terms before booking.
Is October too late for a boat tour?
Not necessarily: many days are calm and warm, but keep a spare day in case of cancellation.
When is the best time to charter a yacht on the Amalfi Coast?
June and September: warm sea, settled weather and better availability and prices than July and August.
What time of day is best for a boat tour?
Morning, when the sea is usually calmest. For photos of Positano, a late afternoon or sunset trip has the best light.
