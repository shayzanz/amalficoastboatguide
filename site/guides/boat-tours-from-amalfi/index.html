Boat tours from Amalfi

Amalfi is the eastern hub of the coast, ideal if you stay in Amalfi, Atrani, Ravello, Minori or Maiori.

Updated November 2026 · By the Amalfi Coast Boat Guide team

From Amalfi, take the shared Capri tour for a full classic day, or a private three to four hour cruise to the Emerald Grotto, Furore and Praiano if you prefer calm water and swimming.

Where boats leave from

Tours start from Amalfi harbour, next to the ferry piers on the seafront by Piazza Flavio Gioia. Guests from Ravello usually take a bus or taxi down, so allow for traffic in summer.

Capri from Amalfi

The crossing to Capri is longer than from Positano or Sorrento, roughly an hour and a half each way, but you see the entire coast on the way. Shared tours make it a long but rich day. See route. On a private boat, a speedboat makes the distance much easier.

The coast from Amalfi

The stretch between Amalfi and Positano holds the coast's best sights by sea: the Emerald Grotto, the Furore fjord and the beaches of Praiano. A private half-day cruise covers it comfortably and can include Positano from the water.

Tips

If Capri is your priority and you have flexibility, consider starting from Positano to gain an hour on the island. Amalfi tours are often less crowded than Positano ones in July and August.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Capri from Amalfi by boat?
Around 35 km of sea, about 75 to 90 minutes on a tour boat and faster on a speedboat.
Can I visit the Emerald Grotto by boat from Amalfi?
Yes, many private and some shared boats stop there. Entry into the grotto itself is a separate ticket.
Is Ravello a good base for boat tours?
Yes, via Amalfi harbour, about 20 minutes away by bus or taxi.
