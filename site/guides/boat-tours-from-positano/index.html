Boat tours from Positano

Positano is the most popular base for boat days, with tours leaving straight from the beach.

Updated November 2026 · By the Amalfi Coast Boat Guide team

From Positano the best full-day option is a small-group Capri boat tour; with only an evening, take the sunset cruise to Li Galli. Groups and families should price a private gozzo.

Where boats leave from

Almost all tours start from the pier on Spiaggia Grande, the main beach, a short walk down from the centre. Operators have kiosks along the beach. Arrive 15 minutes early; in summer the pier is busy and boats leave on time.

Your options

  • Shared Capri day (7–8 hours): the most booked tour in the area. See route and details.
  • Sunset cruise (2–3 hours): Li Galli, a swim and an aperitivo. Details.
  • Private boat to Capri: same route, your pace. Details.
  • Private coast cruise: Praiano, Furore and the Emerald Grotto in a half day. Details.

Tips

Book the Capri day for early in your stay so you have a spare day if the sea is rough. Pack cash for the Blue Grotto and the funicular, a hat and reef-safe sunscreen. If you are prone to seasickness, sit at the back of the boat and choose a morning departure, when the sea is usually calmer.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the boat ride from Positano to Capri?
About 45 to 60 minutes in a typical tour boat, depending on the boat and the sea.
Can I go from Positano to Capri without a tour?
Yes, by public ferry in season. A tour adds the lap of the island and swim stops that the ferry does not.
Do Positano boat tours run in winter?
Very few. The season runs roughly from April to October.
