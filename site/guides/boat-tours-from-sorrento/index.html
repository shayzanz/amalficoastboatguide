Boat tours from Sorrento

Sorrento has the most departures and the shortest crossing to Capri.

Updated November 2026 · By the Amalfi Coast Boat Guide team

From Sorrento, a shared Capri tour gives the most island time for the money. If you want to see the Amalfi Coast itself, take the boat day to Positano and Amalfi instead of the coast road.

Where boats leave from

Most tours leave from Marina Piccola, below the town centre, reached by stairs or a lift from the Villa Comunale. Some include hotel pickups along the Sorrento peninsula.

Two very different days

Capri is under an hour away, so a Sorrento Capri tour spends more time around the island. The Amalfi Coast is on the other side of the peninsula: a boat day to Positano and Amalfi rounds Punta Campanella and avoids the slow coast road.

Which first?

If you only have one day, most people choose Capri. With two, do both, Capri first in case you need a spare day for weather.

Tips

Sorrento has many operators, so compare group size and inclusions carefully. Check whether the Punta Campanella marine reserve and the Bay of Ieranto are on the route: they are some of the most beautiful swim spots in the area.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get from Sorrento to Capri by boat?
About 30 to 50 minutes depending on the boat.
Is it better to see the Amalfi Coast by boat or by bus from Sorrento?
By boat if you can: it avoids traffic and gives the classic views of Positano from the sea.
Do Sorrento boat tours include hotel pickup?
Some do. Check the listing details before booking.
