Private vs shared boat tour on the Amalfi Coast

Most people decide this first, and it changes everything else about the day.

Updated November 2026 · By the Amalfi Coast Boat Guide team

Choose a shared small-group tour if you are one to three people on a normal budget. Choose a private boat for families with young children, groups of six or more, special occasions or if you want to control the timing.

The real difference

Both follow similar routes. What changes is who decides. On a shared tour the plan is fixed: departure time, swim stops, time ashore. On a private boat the skipper works for you, so you can leave early to beat the crowds at the Blue Grotto, stay longer at a cove you like or skip Capri town entirely.

Indicative ranges. From around six people, a private boat is often close to the shared price per head.

Choose shared if

  • you are travelling alone, as a couple or as a small group on a budget;
  • you enjoy meeting other travellers;
  • you are happy with a set plan.

Choose private if

  • you have children who need shade, snacks and shorter stretches;
  • it is a honeymoon, proposal or birthday;
  • you want a specific beach club lunch or an early Blue Grotto attempt;
  • you are six or more.

What to check before booking either

Group size (small group means roughly 12 or fewer), what drinks and snorkel gear are included, whether fuel and port fees are extra on private boats, and the cancellation policy for bad weather.

Frequently asked questions

Is a private boat tour on the Amalfi Coast worth the money?
For groups of six or more, families and special occasions, usually yes: per person it costs little more than shared seats and the day is far more flexible.
How many people fit on a private gozzo?
Typical gozzo boats take 8 to 12 passengers. Larger groups need a bigger boat or two boats.
Can I share a private boat with strangers?
Some operators sell seats on small "semi-private" boats. Ask us on WhatsApp if you want to be matched.

Want a tailor-made boat day?

Send us your dates, how many you are and where you are staying. We answer on WhatsApp, and we also know a few skippers who do not sell online.
