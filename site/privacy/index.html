Privacy and cookies

This website is operated by amalficoastboatguide.com. Contact: WhatsApp +39 393 855 2086.

What we collect

We have no user accounts and we do not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (Netlify) processes technical data such as IP address in server logs to deliver the site securely.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

Only if you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) to count visits and see which pages and booking links people use, so we know what to improve. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses, and we do not use it for advertising or share the data with ad networks. Data is kept for at most 14 months. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all. See Google's privacy policy.

Third-party content

  • GetYourGuide widget: loads only after you accept. GetYourGuide may set cookies to show tours and attribute bookings. See GetYourGuide's privacy policy.
  • Affiliate links to GetYourGuide and Viator: when you click, those sites may set cookies under their own policies.
  • Maps: route maps load tiles from OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address.
  • WhatsApp: if you contact us, your messages are processed by WhatsApp (Meta) and by us only to answer you.

Your choices

You can change your cookie choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. Under the GDPR you may request access to or deletion of data you sent us by contacting us.
