Capri boat tour from Positano (shared)

The classic full day: Li Galli, a lap of Capri with swim stops and free time on the island.

Typical route

  1. Positano
  2. Li Galli islands
  3. Punta Campanella
  4. Faraglioni rocks
  5. Marina Piccola
  6. Punta Carena lighthouse
  7. Blue Grotto
  8. Capri (Marina Grande)
  9. Positano

Boats leave from the pier on Positano's main beach in the morning, pass the Li Galli islands and cross to Capri past Punta Campanella. Most of the morning is a slow lap of the island: the Faraglioni rocks and the arch, the green and white grottoes, the Punta Carena lighthouse, and a stop outside the Blue Grotto if it is open. You then get two to four hours ashore at Marina Grande to ride the funicular up to the Piazzetta or have lunch, before sailing back in the late afternoon.

Small-group boats (roughly 10 to 15 people) are noticeably more pleasant than large ones. Drinks and snorkelling gear are often included, lunch on Capri usually is not.

A small detail people miss: the three Li Galli islands were bought by the dancer Léonide Massine in the 1920s and later by Rudolf Nureyev. They are still private, so you see them from the boat only.

What works

  • Covers almost every Capri highlight in one day
  • Positano is close enough to Capri for an easy crossing
  • Several swim stops in clear water
  • Good value per person

Before you book

  • Blue Grotto entry is extra and depends on the sea
  • Free time on Capri is busy in July and August
  • Large boats mean less flexibility
