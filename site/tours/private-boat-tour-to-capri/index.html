Your own boat and skipper for the day: Capri at your pace, with swims where you want.

  6. Punta Carena lighthouse
  7. Blue Grotto

  9. Nerano bay
  10. Positano

A private day follows the same basic route as the shared tours, but the skipper adapts it to you: longer swims at the Faraglioni, lunch at a beach club in Marina Piccola or Anacapri, an early start to beat the queue at the Blue Grotto. Departures are possible from Positano, Amalfi, Praiano or Sorrento.

Price depends mostly on the boat. A traditional wooden gozzo with a sun canopy is the classic choice, a speedboat is faster and better from Amalfi, a yacht costs considerably more. Split between six or eight people, a gozzo is often not much more per head than a shared tour.

Ask your skipper about lunch near Bagni di Tiberio, west of Marina Grande, where you swim next to the remains of a Roman villa.

What works

  • Completely flexible itinerary
  • Best for families, honeymoons and groups
  • Skipper doubles as a local guide

Before you book

  • Fuel and port fees may be extra
  • Clarify what drinks and food are included
  • Book early for July and August
